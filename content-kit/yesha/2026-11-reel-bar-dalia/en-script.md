# Bar Dalia – English script (final v2, client's wording, as in bar-dalia-english.mp4)

For about 1,500 years, there was no Jewish village here.   (on-screen opener)

A Jewish village from the Talmudic era is coming back to life.
West of the Jordan Valley checkpoint stands a farm that tells a story of past, present and future.
According to tradition, a small Jewish village called Bar Dalia once stood here – home to the Delaiah priestly course, which is also mentioned in the Talmud.
After the terror attack at Mehola Junction in August 2024, in which Yonatan Deutsch was killed, the farm was established.
The goal was to strengthen our presence in the area and create a barrier between villages in northern Samaria where terrorists operate and the Jordan Valley.
Today, Uri and Ashchar Rosenfeld, together with their son Aniam, are building a pioneering farm here, with a vision of founding a beit midrash – a house of Torah study – that joins Torah and labor, spirit and soil.
Today, the farm safeguards hundreds of dunams, working the land and breathing life into the area.
