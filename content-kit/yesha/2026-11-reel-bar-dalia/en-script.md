# Bar Dalia – English version (draft for review with Oren)

## Voice-over
1. A Jewish village from the Talmudic era is coming back to life.
2. West of the Jordan Valley checkpoint stands a farm that tells a story of past, present and future.
3. According to tradition, a small Jewish village called Bar Dalia once stood here – home to the Delaiah priestly course, which is also mentioned in the Talmud.
4. After the terror attack at Mehola Junction, in which Yonatan Deutsch, of blessed memory, was killed, the farm was established out of a strategic need: to strengthen our hold on the area, and to block territorial continuity between the villages of northern Samaria and the Jordan Valley.
5. Uri and Ashchar Rosenfeld, together with their son Aniam, are building a pioneering settlement here, with a vision of founding a beit midrash – a house of Torah study – that joins Torah and labor, spirit and soil.
6. Today, the farm safeguards hundreds of dunams, working the land and breathing life into the area.
